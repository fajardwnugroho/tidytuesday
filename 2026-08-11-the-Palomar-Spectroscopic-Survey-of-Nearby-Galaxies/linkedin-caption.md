## Option 3 — science-narrative

> "Half of the objects are star-forming nuclei, the other half some form of
> AGN." — Ho, Filippenko & Sargent (1997).
>
> That's the finding of the Palomar Spectroscopic Survey: **active black holes
> are common** in the local universe. For this week's #TidyTuesday I re-derived
> it from the raw spectra data:
>
> 1. **Two line ratios** ([OIII]/Hβ vs [NII]/Hα) cleanly separate galaxies
>    powered by stars from ones powered by accretion disks — the BPT diagram.
> 2. **Galaxy shape matters**: 73% of ellipticals host LINER nuclei; 59% of
>    spirals are star-forming.
> 3. **Mass is the master variable**: median velocity dispersion climbs from
>    70 km/s (star-forming) to 167 km/s (LINERs) — the M-sigma relation.
>
> Charts in R; write-up in the comments.
